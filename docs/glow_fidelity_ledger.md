## Remaining Deviations

- The local and global world models are typed symbolic artifacts plus prompt-mediated analysis, not separately trained neural world models.
- The archive is now independent from the frontier, but it is still symbolic and non-learned; there is no separately evolving learned memory model yet.
- Selection, reranking, and fallback behavior remain highly inspectable and config-weighted rather than learned end-to-end.
- JSONL trajectory writing is preserved because it is practical for Jericho debugging, even though the paper conceptually reasons over complete trajectory artifacts.
- Archived states keep exact restore handles, but when a source trajectory has fallen out of the frontier the runner may no longer retain the full original provenance prefix text for frontier-facing branch trajectory reconstruction.
- Native Jericho snapshot references are preferred but not always fully serializable; replay fallback remains part of the runtime contract.
